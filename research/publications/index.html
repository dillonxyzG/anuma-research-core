ANUMA · Publications & Research Archive

Research papers, frameworks and working documents

The ANUMA research archive documents the development of the theoretical, mathematical and experimental programme surrounding Resonance Transformation Theory, Transactional–Relational Intelligence, Existential Intelligence and Living Intelligence Systems.

The archive is intended to preserve the progression from conceptual formulation to mathematical modelling, experimental design and future empirical investigation.

01 · Research Archive

A living record of the research programme

ANUMA treats research documents as evolving records rather than isolated publications. Papers may therefore represent foundational frameworks, mathematical developments, experimental proposals, technical notes or later empirical results.

Archive principle: conceptual claims, proposed mathematical models and experimentally supported findings should remain distinguishable throughout the research record.

Foundational Framework

Mathematical Foundations of Resonance Transformation Theory

A teaching and research framework for Resonance Transformation Theory, Existential Intelligence and Living Intelligence Systems. The framework develops formal representations of system state, relational structure, memory, context, agency and resonance, together with proposed experimental and information-theoretic tests.

04 · Document Structure

Every research document should remain auditable

The archive is designed so that future readers can distinguish what a document proposes, what it derives, what it tests and what evidence it reports.

Proposition

What conceptual or theoretical claim is being proposed?

Formalisation

What mathematical or computational structure represents the proposed relationship?

Hypothesis

What prediction can be investigated experimentally?

Method

What experimental or analytical procedure could test the hypothesis?

Evidence

What observations, measurements or results support or challenge the proposed model?

Revision

How does new evidence modify the research framework?

06 · Scientific Positioning

Research status remains explicit

The archive maintains a distinction between established scientific foundations, proposed theoretical frameworks and hypotheses awaiting empirical investigation.

Established Science

Mathematics, information theory, network science, dynamical systems, complex systems and related scientific fields provide established foundations from which the research proceeds.

Proposed Framework

ANUMA develops proposed relationships between state, context, memory, relationships, agency and resonance.

Testable Hypotheses

Claims concerning relational information, transformation and emergent intelligence are framed as questions for empirical investigation.
